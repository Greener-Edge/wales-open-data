Greener Edge open data

Four free tools built on official, openly licensed data: carbon and buildings in Wales, the climate risks in the UK's food supply, and a checker for your own ingredients.

Wales emissions

Wales has cut its greenhouse gas emissions by 46% since 2005. A cleaner grid did more of the work than industrial closures, but last year's fall was almost all Port Talbot.

Open the emissions map

Commercial EPC

From 2031, large privately rented commercial buildings are set to need an EPC B. Today, 59% of large buildings in Wales fall short.

Open the EPC map

UK food risk

Search any food to see where the UK imports it from and the water, drought, heat and deforestation risks it carries. Compare that with how much the UK grows itself, and explore Welsh farming since 1867.

Open UK food risk

Supply chain checker

A free tool for food and drink businesses. List your ingredients and where they come from, and see which face water stress, drought, flooding, rising heat or deforestation risk. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
