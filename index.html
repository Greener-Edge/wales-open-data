Where did Wales's emissions go?

Wales has cut its greenhouse gas emissions by 46% since 2005. A cleaner grid did more of the work than industrial closures, but last year's fall was almost all Port Talbot.

Is Wales's commercial property ready for EPC B?

From 2031, large privately rented commercial buildings are set to need an EPC B. Today, 59% of large buildings in Wales fall short.
