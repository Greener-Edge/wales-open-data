Check your supply chain

List the ingredients you buy and where they come from. In seconds you'll see which face water stress, drought, flooding, rising heat or deforestation risk, and where you depend on one country.

Your data stays with you. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored by Greener Edge. Your list is saved in this browser only, so you can come back to it.

Your ingredients

Start typing an ingredient and pick from the list. Choose where it comes from, or leave it as "Origin unknown" to use the UK's average import mix for that food. Spend is optional: if you add it, results are weighted by what you spend.

How it works: each origin is scored using the same open datasets as our food imports map: WRI Aqueduct 4.0 for water stress, drought and river flooding (CC BY 4.0); the World Bank Climate Change Knowledge Portal for projected warming (ODbL); Singh et al. (2026) for deforestation risk linked to UK imports of each crop from each country (CC BY); and FAOSTAT 2024 for the UK import mix. A flag means: high or extremely high water stress, medium-high or high drought risk, high or extremely high river flood risk, or that UK imports of that crop from that country are linked to deforestation. Full details are on the Methods page.

Limits: scores are country averages, so they can't reflect a specific farm, region or certification. Deforestation flags show risk exposure, not proof. This is a screening tool, not an audit.
