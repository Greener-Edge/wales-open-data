Your results in brief

The report opens your browser's print window: choose "Save as PDF". Nothing is uploaded.

Where your ingredients come from

What this shows: supply lines from each origin to the UK, and a flow chart linking each ingredient to its origin. Thickness shows your spend, or the number of ingredients if spend isn't entered; colour shows the main risk at that origin.

Why it matters: it shows at a glance where your purchasing is concentrated, and which routes carry the most risk.

Ingredient by ingredient

What this shows: each ingredient's risks at its chosen origin: water stress today and in the future, drought, river flooding, projected warming and deforestation, with an overall rating.

Why it matters: it tells you which ingredients and suppliers to talk to first.

Questions to ask your suppliers

Generated from your results. Use them as prompts, not a checklist.

How it works

Each origin is scored using the same open datasets as our UK food risk site. A flag means high or extremely high water stress, medium-high or high drought risk, high or extremely high river flood risk, or that UK imports of that crop from that country are linked to deforestation (10 ha or more in 2023; 500 ha for the UK import mix).

Results are weighted by spend when spend is entered for every ingredient; otherwise each ingredient counts equally. Supplier questions are prompts based on the flags, not advice.

Limits

Scores are country averages, so they can't reflect a specific farm, region or certification. Deforestation flags show risk exposure, not proof. This is a screening tool, not an audit, a footprint or a compliance assessment.
